Generated by `.venv/bin/python -m atmo_scoops.studies.packing_screen`. This checks saved numerical load cases against a named reference packaging figure; it is not a new payoff run or an optimizer capability. See [source evidence](../solar/PACKAGING.md).

The cylinder is deliberately generous: no head/wall/rib losses are subtracted. Mission bus power is equated to source rated power and no repeat-cycle mechanism, protective bay or external clearance is added. A real rated-power mapping may demand more array. The reference 40 kW/m³ is a published design figure, not a fundamental maximum for future technology.

For the 937 km-apogee case, reference array packaging alone exceeds the entire hull. For the deeper joint-boundary case, arrays plus retained warm-gas volume already exceed the hull. This invalidates claiming that either saved case fits using this reference packaging. It does not establish infeasibility for every future technology, power system or collection policy.

The required packing numbers leave **no room** for any other hardware or front/interstage gas. They are demand thresholds for this load/layout, not proposed technology coefficients. Metal material volume excludes rack gas passages, frames and deployment hardware. Blade-row length excludes cooler chambers, buffers and drives; it does not justify the previous 100 m bay or prove that a 22 m bay suffices.
